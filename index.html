Programme progression

Weeks 1–4: technique and working weights. Weeks 5–8: increase reps or weight. Weeks 9–11: continue progression. Week 12: deload by approximately 30–40%.

Aim to finish most working sets with 1–2 good reps left. When the top of the target rep range feels comfortable, increase weight slightly.
